## Tips for Finding Images:

1. Visit the DOI link for each paper
2. Look for:
   - Graphical abstract
   - First figure from the paper
   - Featured image
3. Right-click and save as the filename above
4. Recommended size: 400x400px or similar square/rectangular format
5. Format: PNG preferred (or JPG)
